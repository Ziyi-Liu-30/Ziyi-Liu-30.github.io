About

I am a Professor at Soochow University. From December 2023 to December 2025, I was a Research Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Queensland in 2023. My research focuses on spatiotemporal data intelligence, with applications in urban transportation, digital twins, and embodied AI. I have served as a reviewer and program committee member for multiple top-tier international journals and conferences in databases and artificial intelligence.
